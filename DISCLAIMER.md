# Disclaimer

This project is intended for defensive cybersecurity education, authorized phishing analysis, and portfolio demonstration.

Do not:

- Visit live malicious URLs directly from a personal or production browser.
- Execute suspicious files on a personal or production computer.
- Upload confidential company files, emails, customer information, or credentials to public analysis platforms without authorization.
- Attempt to gain access to systems, accounts, or infrastructure you do not own or have explicit permission to test.

Use sanitized indicators, passive intelligence, remote rendering services, isolated sandboxes, and organization-approved procedures.
